# Code of Conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant v2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

## Standards

- Be respectful and constructive.
- Focus on the code, not the person.
- Assume good intent.
- Disagree technically, not personally.

## Enforcement

Report violations to **administrador@tealquilamos.com**. Reports are treated confidentially.

Maintainers may warn, edit, or remove contributions that violate this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban contributors for repeated or severe violations.
